Applied AI to solve real business problems — from sanctions risk monitoring to process automation.

Risk & Compliance
Sanctions Breach Monitoring Tool
Designed an AI-powered monitoring tool to detect potential sanctions evasion across a multi-tier distributor network. The tool analyses business volume trends, flags anomalies such as sudden spikes from new or existing channel partners, and maps the full sales chain from authorised distributors to end consumers. Each entity receives an AI-generated risk score, identifying those with the highest likelihood of diverting products to sanctioned markets or unauthorised channels.
Outcome — High-risk entities prioritised for targeted investigation and fed into the annual audit roadmap.
Process Automation
Automated Partner Communication Workflow
Built an AI-driven automation to replace a manual, quarterly process where account managers individually prepared and distributed financial templates to channel partners across a regional network. The solution auto-generates the correct documents, distributes them on schedule, and sends confirmation notifications back to the account manager — removing the need for manual intervention entirely.
Outcome — Eliminated repetitive manual effort, ensured on-time delivery, and reduced errors across the partner network.
